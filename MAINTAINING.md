# Updating the Yemmireddy Lab website

A guide for lab members. **You do not need to know any code.** Everything is
edited through a web form, and the website updates itself 1–2 minutes after you
click **Save**.

---

## 1. One-time setup (2 minutes)

1. The site administrator invites you **by email** from the admin panel. You do
   **not** need a GitHub account.
2. Open the invitation email and click the sign-in link.
3. You'll land in the admin panel with a menu on the left:
   *People, Alumni, News, Publications, Page: Home, …*

Next time, go to **<https://yemmireddylab.com/admin>** (it takes you to the
admin panel at app.pagescms.org), enter your email, and click the sign-in link
it sends you. Bookmark that address — it's the only one you need.

---

## 2. Common tasks

### Add a new lab member
1. **People → Add an entry**.
2. Fill in **Full name**, choose the **Group** (e.g. *Graduate Student*), and
   type their **Position / title**.
3. **Photo**: click the image field → **Upload** → choose a photo.
   *Portrait photos with the face near the top look best. JPG or PNG only —
   iPhone "HEIC" photos must be exported as JPG first (on a Mac: open in
   Preview → File → Export → JPEG).*
4. Add **Education**, **Research interests**, and **Current research** items.
   Use the **+ Add** button for each extra line.
5. **Sort order** controls who appears first within a group (smaller = earlier).
6. Click **Save**.

### Move someone to Alumni (when they graduate)
1. **Alumni** → under *Graduate student alumni* (or *Undergraduate alumni*)
   click **+ Add**, fill in name, degree & year, and current position. **Save**.
2. **People** → open the person → **⋯ menu → Delete**.

### Post news
1. **News → Add an entry**.
2. Fill in **Headline**, **Date**, and a one- or two-sentence **Short summary**.
3. Optional: a **Cover photo** plus a short description of the photo.
4. Write the story in the **Story** box (bold, links, lists and images are supported).
5. Tick **Draft** if you want to save without publishing yet.
6. **Save**. The three latest news items also appear on the home page.

### Add a publication
1. **Publications → Add an entry**.
2. Title, Authors, Journal, Year — and paste the **DOI** (e.g. `10.1016/j.foodcont.2024.110000`).
3. **Save**. Publications are grouped by year automatically and are searchable.

### Change the home-page photos
**Page: Home → Rotating photos**. Add, remove, or drag to reorder. Wide
(landscape) photos work best; 3–5 is ideal. Always fill in *Photo description*.

### Update contact details, email, or phone
**Site settings & contact**. These appear in the footer and on the Contact page.

### Edit text on Research, Teaching, or Training & Outreach
Open **Page: Research** (or *Teaching*, *Training & Outreach*) and edit the
fields. In long text boxes, **leave an empty line between paragraphs**.

---

## 3. Good habits

- **Always describe photos** ("Photo description"). It's required for visitors
  who use screen readers, and it helps Google find the site.
- **Photo size doesn't matter.** Upload phone or camera photos as they are —
  large photos are shrunk automatically a minute or two after you save.
- **You can't break the site.** If something is filled in incorrectly (e.g. a
  required field is empty), the update is simply not published and the
  previous version stays online. Ask the administrator to check the
  *Actions* tab on GitHub if a change doesn't appear after 5 minutes.
- **Every change is saved in history.** Mistakes can always be undone by the
  administrator.

| People                      | People + individual profiles  |
| Alumni                      | Bottom of the People page     |
| News                        | News page + home page         |
| Publications                | Publications page             |
| Page: Home                  | Home page                     |
| Page: Research / Teaching / Training & Outreach | Those pages |
| Site settings & contact     | Footer, Contact page, Google  |
